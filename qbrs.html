QBRs and operating rhythm

A quarterly business review per agency per quarter: held, due or overdue. The QBR pack is written from the record, headline figures, trend, variance reasons, actions with owners and next-quarter commitments, and prints on one or two pages. Roadshows and enablement sessions are logged with the adoption they produced. All data is invented.

Rules

A QBR is overdue when its due date has passed and no held date is recorded. The grace period only affects the "due soon" count.
