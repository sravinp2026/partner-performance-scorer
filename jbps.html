Joint business plans

Each agency signs a JBP a year: committed spend by quarter and market, the formats and pilots agreed, the terms. Attainment is measured against that commitment, not an internal target. Edit an attainment figure, pick a reason for a variance, and every roll-up recomputes. All data is invented.

Rules editable

On plan means attainment within the band either side of 100%. Below the attainment floor the row gets a recovery action for the partner manager. A variance outside the band with no reason code gets a "pick a reason" action, so no gap stays unexplained.
