Partner Performance Scorer

Scores each agency partner 0–100 from five commercial signals, explains every score, flags thin or stale data instead of scoring it, and tracks the agreed actions to closure. Edit any cell, weight or rule and everything recomputes. All data is invented.

Weights sum shown below

Signals are scaled 0–1 before weighting. A signal whose inputs are missing is not scored; its weight is redistributed across the others and the gap is shown, never filled with a zero.

Rules thresholds behind the actions

Every recommended action is one of these thresholds being crossed. Change a rule and the action list updates, so the team can agree the rules once and argue about numbers, not opinions.

Method, and what was learnt building it

No invented figures. A missing input leaves the signal unscored and visibly blank. A zero is a real zero. This is the rule that kept Keyva's property data trustworthy, and it is the rule here.

Thin data is flagged, not averaged away. Growth on a base under the floor (see rules) is unreliable and is marked low confidence. A partner with fewer than two quarters is scored but shown as "new", and figures older than the staleness limit are flagged for a refresh before the QBR.

Like with like. Scores compare one quarter with the same partner's previous quarter. The ranking never mixes quarters, and holding-group totals only add partners that have a figure for the review quarter.

Every number explains itself. "Why this score" shows the raw inputs, the scaled signal, the weight and the threshold behind each action, so a QBR starts from evidence rather than a label.

Actions are tracked, not just recommended. Each action has an owner (the partner manager) and a checkbox; the open count sits in the headline figures. Changes to inputs, weights and rules are logged.
