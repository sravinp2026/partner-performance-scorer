Partner Performance Scorer

Scores each agency partner 0–100 from five commercial signals, sorts them into tiers, and recommends the next action. Edit any cell or weight and everything recomputes. Dummy data only.

Weights

Signals are scaled 0–1 before weighting: attainment hits 1.0 at 120% of target, growth spans −20%…+30% quarter on quarter, breadth hits 1.0 at 15 active advertisers, adoption blends formats used (70%) and beta pilots (30%), pipeline hits 1.0 at 1.5× next-quarter target.

QBR narrative

Pick a partner. The app drafts the quarterly summary from the numbers above, and gives you the same facts as a prompt you can paste into Claude or ChatGPT for a longer narrative. The model only writes around the numbers; it never invents them.

Method and design choices

Why a weighted sum and plain rules. Partner managers can argue with a weight; they cannot argue with a black box. Every recommended action traces to one number crossing one threshold, so a QBR can start from "here is why".

Actions map to motions the team already runs: JBP reset, QBR, roadshow for lapsed clients, format enablement, beta-pilot invitation, pipeline-planning review, target raise and multi-market expansion.

What I would do next with real data: pull revenue and pipeline from the CRM nightly, add holding-group and market roll-ups to the leadership review, track score deltas quarter on quarter, and use an LLM for the narrative only.
