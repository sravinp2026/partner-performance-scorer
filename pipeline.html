Pipeline and coverage

Every opportunity by agency, advertiser, market and stage. Coverage is open pipeline expected next quarter divided by next quarter's JBP commitment, by agency and by market. Deals not updated for N days are flagged stale; an agency with nothing in the CRM shows a blank, never a zero. All data is invented.

Stage probabilities editable

Weighted value = value × stage probability. Change a probability and the weighted figures update; the change is logged.

Rules

A stale deal is one whose as-of date is older than the limit. Coverage below the floor gets a pipeline-planning action for the partner manager.
